Services

Integrity, fracture, materials, welding, and inspection.

Codes of record include ASME BPVC, ASTM, NACE, DNV, and API. Work covers onshore and offshore systems. Each heading is a separate page.

  1. 01

    Pipeline integrity & corrosion

    Internal CO2, H2S, and MIC; external CP; erosion; coatings; and remaining-wall FFS.

  2. 02

    Fracture mechanics & FFS

    API 579 and BS 7910 assessments of wall-loss, surface indications, and fracture in welded assemblies.

  3. 03

    Materials & mill qualification

    Sweet and sour selection, bilingual specifications, and mill qualification worldwide.

  4. 04

    Welding technology & HAZ

    WPS evaluation, NDE systems, HAZ toughness, and steel chemistry effects on weld performance.

  5. 05

    Specialized inspection of critical components

    Inspection and NDE review scoped to the component that actually carries the risk.
