Tools

Two working tools, issued to clients.

These are not public calculators and not a product catalog. Access is provided in the context of an engagement. Both run as standalone web applications.

Client application

MIC Risk Evaluator

Microbiologically influenced corrosion is not a single coupon reading. It is a risk argument that has to combine water chemistry, operating conditions, materials, and the plausibility of microbial activity in the line or facility under review.

The MIC Risk Evaluator is a client-access tool used in that assessment. A login is required. Feature lists and screenshots are not published here; the tool is demonstrated in the course of the work.

Open MIC Risk Evaluator

Client application

Steel Chemistry / HAZ Toughness Predictor

HAZ toughness is sensitive to chemistry in ways that a WPS table does not always make obvious. This predictor estimates HAZ toughness from steel chemistry so welding procedure and material decisions can be screened before a procedure is locked and before a mill heat is accepted on toughness grounds alone.

It is a decision-support estimate, not a substitute for qualification testing or production impact tests. Access is provided to clients.
