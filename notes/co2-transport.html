CO2 transport

A working note on materials and integrity for dense-phase CO2 pipeline transport. The full text is published as a Sway document; this page is the site record and the links.

The note is the public trail for the same questions that appear on CCUS transport work: decompression, ductile-fracture control, corrosion, and the role of composite crack arrestors where toughness alone may not arrest a running fracture. It sits alongside the PRCI REX2023 dense-phase CO2 record.

Related: Aquiles Perez and Rick Hill / Richard Hill, “Technical Considerations for CO2 Pipeline Transport and the Role of Composite Crack Arrestors,” PHMSA R&D Forum, 14 September 2023. C. Alexander, A. Perez, and C. Whalen, “Thoughts Towards an Effective Crack Arrestor Design for CO2 Pipelines,” PPIM 2024 (API RP 1192, Reference 25).
