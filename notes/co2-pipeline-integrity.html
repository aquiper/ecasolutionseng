Insights · Technical overview

Dense-phase CO2 pipeline integrity.

A short overview of the linked 2023 PHMSA presentation.

← All insights

Define the fluid and operating envelope

Consider composition, pressure, temperature, and decompression together with pipe diameter, wall thickness, strength, and toughness. The presentation connects these factors to the resistance needed to arrest a running ductile fracture.

Include corrosion and upset conditions

Water and impurities can affect corrosion. The assessment should account for operating transients and upset conditions, alongside the barriers or mitigation included in the design.

Assess composite crack arrestors in context

The presentation explores composite crack arrestors as a possible part of field-joint installation. It also identifies validation gaps; its research proposals should be read in their 2023 context.

Original source

Presentation by Aquiles Perez and Richard Hill, PHMSA R&D Forum, 14 September 2023.
