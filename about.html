Practice

EC&A Solutions Eng LLC is a Houston-area engineering consultancy led by Aquiles Perez, PhD (Florida Atlantic University). The firm has more than fifteen years of work on oil and gas materials, corrosion, welding, mill qualification, and fitness-for-service — from specification and mill floor through in-service assessment.

Engagements are for operators, EPCs, mills, and class / assurance bodies that need a heat, a WPS, a CP survey, an inspection of a critical component, and a crack-arrest argument read as one problem. Work covers conventional oil and gas as well as CCUS, ammonia, and hydrogen transport.

Specifications are prepared in English and Spanish. Mill qualification is performed worldwide. Specialized inspection of critical components — pipe, welds, coatings, and pressure-containing hardware — is scoped to the item that actually carries the risk. The work is independent consulting: scoped, documented, and written for engineering, integrity, and procurement teams.
