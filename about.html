Who we are

Consultancy Services

EC&A Solutions Eng LLC is a Houston-area engineering consultancy led by Aquiles Perez, PhD. More than fifteen years of work on oil and gas materials, corrosion, welding, mill qualification, and fitness-for-service — from specification and mill floor through in-service assessment.

The work is for operators, EPCs, mills, and class / assurance bodies that need someone who can read a heat, a WPS, a CP FEED evaluation, an inspection of a critical component, and a crack-arrest argument as one problem. Engagements span conventional oil and gas as well as CCUS, ammonia, and hydrogen transport.

Project-tailored specifications for particular requirements: offshore and onshore, high H2S, CO2, dense-phase CO2, H2, ammonia, and chloride, for line pipe, components, forgings, induction bends, and non-metallics. Mill qualification is performed worldwide. Specialized inspection of critical components — pipe, welds, coatings, and pressure-containing hardware — is scoped to the item that actually carries the risk, not as a generic survey. The work is independent consulting: scoped, documented, and written to be used by engineering, integrity, and procurement teams.
