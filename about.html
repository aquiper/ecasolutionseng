Specialized engineering from an independent firm.

Aquiles Perez, PhD
Principal, EC&A Solutions Eng LLC

Ph.D., Florida Atlantic University. Dissertation: Study of Pulsing of Flow Reactants in a Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cell (PEMFC).

Co-chair, API Subcommittee on Corrosion and Materials SCC 17U — subsea wet and dry thermal insulation. Member of NACE (AMPP) and ASTM.

The work is materials, welding, corrosion and integrity — the problems that decide whether a component stays in service, and whether the decision holds up when somebody checks it.

Why independent

An assessment is only useful if the person who has to accept it believes the person who wrote it had no stake in the answer. This practice takes no fabrication work, sells no inspection services, and has no equipment to place. The deliverable is the judgement.
