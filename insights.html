Reference

PHMSA — composite crack arrestors

Presented 14 September 2023 at the PHMSA R&D Forum. The work covers ductile-fracture control and corrosion in dense-phase CO2 transport, and the role of composite crack arrestors as part of the pipeline system — including field-joint installation — where toughness alone may not arrest a running fracture.

Authors: Dr. Aquiles Perez and Richard Hill. This is the same body of work as the CO2 technical note and the PRCI REX2023 paper. Related crack-arrestor work with C. Alexander and C. Whalen is cited as Reference 25 in API RP 1192 (PPIM 2024).
