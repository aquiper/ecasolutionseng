Case study 03 · Mill qualification

Qualifying an ERW mill before a single joint ships.

Operator
Redacted
System
An HF-ERW mill qualifying to supply API 5L PSL 2 line pipe into a project with critical-service requirements.
Question
Could the mill’s process be shown to hold weld integrity and mechanical uniformity across heats, and could its AUT actually be trusted to find what it claimed to find?
Approach
30 pipes from 10 coils, split 5 coils from one heat and 5 from another, so within-heat and between-heat consistency were both exposed. Transverse, longitudinal and cross-weld tensiles on all 30. CVN at weld, fusion line and bond line +0.5 mm, plus DWTT, on 10. Weld macro on every pipe — hourglass symmetry, fibre angle, penetrators, cold welds, hook cracks. Ten pipes sampled at both mother coil and finished pipe to quantify what forming and sizing did to properties.
Finding
AUT verification written as a separate discipline: dedicated calibration standard, 15 consecutive scans at production speed, every reflector detected on every scan, signal response varying no more than 2 dB scan to scan. On review, notch sensitivity tightened from N-10 to N-5 with 1/16 in notch depth — a deliberately harder target than the mill’s standard setup.
