Inspection of critical components

Inspection of critical components, and the translation of inspection output into something a fracture assessment can actually use.

Services

  • NDE technique review and procedure qualification
  • PAUT and phased array data interpretation
  • Indication sizing, characterisation and sentencing
  • Inspection scope definition driven by the governing damage mechanism
  • Review of third party inspection reports

Why this sits next to the assessment work

An indication reported as a length and a depth is not yet an input. Flaw height, position through wall, orientation and sizing uncertainty are what a fracture assessment consumes, and inspection specified without that in mind routinely produces data that cannot answer the question the operator is asking.

Have a specific case? Describe it and you will get a straight answer on whether it is worth an engagement — including if the answer is no.
