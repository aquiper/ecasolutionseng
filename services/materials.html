Materials selection & mill qualification

Material selection for sweet and sour service — pipelines, forgings, valves, induction bends, and nonmetallics — tied to specifications that mills and fabricators can actually purchase against. Qualification follows the pipe onto the mill floor, not just the data book.

Pipeline integrity & corrosion Fracture mechanics & FFS Materials & mill qualification Welding technology & HAZ Specialized inspection
  • Material selection for sweet and sour service: line pipe, forgings, valves, induction bends, and nonmetallics
  • Specifications in English and Spanish for flowlines, valves, bends, welding, NDE, flanges, fittings, coatings, and CP
  • Mill qualification worldwide: SAWH, LSAW, JCOE, three-roll bend, UOE, and HFERW
  • Statistical assessment of mechanical, chemical, and dimensional properties of pipes, plates, and coils
  • TMCP plate evaluation
  • Personnel and laboratory / NDE system evaluation
