API 5L · NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 · DNV-ST-F101

Materials, corrosion and mill qualification

Materials selection, corrosion assessment and qualification of line pipe — for operators buying, and for mills qualifying to supply. The codes of record are API 5L, NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 and DNV-ST-F101.

Materials and corrosion

  • Damage mechanism review to API 571
  • Materials selection and corrosion allowance, judged at design conditions rather than expected ones
  • Sour service qualification to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156
  • Wet and dense-phase CO2 compatibility
  • Hydrogen and ammonia compatibility, including embrittlement susceptibility
  • Corrosion inhibitor and monitoring programme review

Mill qualification

  • Mill capability audits
  • Manufacturing procedure qualification test design, witness and review
  • Purchase specification development and supplementary requirement selection
  • Mill test certificate review and non-conformance disposition
  • Third party surveillance during production

Both sides of the table

This practice has worked for operators specifying pipe and for mills qualifying to supply it. A specification written by someone who has never had to meet one tends to contain requirements that cost money and buy nothing.
