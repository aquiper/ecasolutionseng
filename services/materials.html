Materials & mills

Mill qualification for LSAW and line pipe.

Qualification of forming route, heat, and test systems, with material selection and specifications mills can purchase against.

← All services

Material selection for sweet and sour service covers line pipe, forgings, valves, induction bends, and nonmetallics, with specifications mills and fabricators can purchase against. Mill qualification covers the heat, forming route, and test system before pipe is in the ditch or on the barge.

  • Material selection for sweet and sour service: line pipe, forgings, valves, induction bends, and nonmetallics
  • Project-tailored specifications for offshore and onshore service, including high H2S, CO2, dense-phase CO2, H2, ammonia, and chloride
  • Mill qualification worldwide: SAWH, LSAW, JCOE, three-roll bend, UOE, and HFERW
  • Statistical assessment of mechanical, chemical, and dimensional properties of pipes, plates, and coils
  • TMCP plate evaluation
  • Personnel and laboratory / NDE system evaluation

Codes of record include ASME, ASTM, NACE, DNV, and API, applied as the project specifies.
