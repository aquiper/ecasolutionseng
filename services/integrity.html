API 579-1 / ASME FFS-1

Integrity and fitness-for-service

A fitness-for-service assessment answers one question: can this equipment stay in service, and under what conditions. The code of record is API 579-1/ASME FFS-1.

What gets assessed

  • General and local metal loss
  • Pitting and localised corrosion
  • Crack-like flaws, including weld toe and HAZ indications
  • Dents, gouges and mechanical damage
  • Hydrogen damage, blistering and HIC
  • Creep, fire damage and laminations

Levels

Level 1 screens against tabulated limits. Level 2 applies the failure assessment diagram with code-default toughness. Level 3 uses measured material data, finite element analysis, or a probabilistic treatment where the deterministic answer is too conservative to be useful.

Most engagements start at Level 1 and stop as soon as the answer is clear. Paying for Level 3 when Level 2 already passes is waste.

What you receive

A written assessment naming the code edition, the input data and its provenance, every assumption made, and the resulting margin.

Have a specific case? Describe it and you will get a straight answer on whether it is worth an engagement — including if the answer is no.
