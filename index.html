Materials, integrity, welding, and inspection

Corrosion, fracture, mill metallurgy, weld HAZ, and inspection of critical components.

  1. 01

    Pipeline integrity & corrosion

    Internal CO2, H2S, and MIC; external CP; erosion; coatings; and FFS for wall loss — written so operations can act.
  2. 02

    Fracture mechanics & FFS

    API 579 and BS 7910 assessments of wall-loss, surface indications, and fracture in pressure-containing welded assemblies.
  3. 03

    Materials & mill qualification

    Sweet and sour selection, bilingual specifications, and mill qualification across SAWH, LSAW, JCOE, UOE, HFERW, and related routes.
  4. 04

    Welding technology & HAZ

    WPS evaluation, NDE systems, HAZ toughness, and steel chemistry effects on weld performance — including a client predictor tool.
  5. 05

    Specialized inspection of critical components

    Inspection and NDE review of pipe, welds, coatings, and pressure-containing hardware — scoped to the component that actually carries the risk.

Typical problems

Sour-service HIC. A mill heat that will not sit still. A weld procedure or HAZ toughness argument. A CO2 or hydrogen fracture-control case. Inspection of the component that actually carries the risk.

Engineering tools

HIC FFS Tool
